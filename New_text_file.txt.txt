**Objective**

The objective of this E-commerce Management System is to help customers purchase products online easily and efficiently. It allows users to explore products, place orders, make payments, and manage their purchases.

The system maintains information about customers, products, orders, and payments using a relational database. It uses primary keys, foreign keys, unique constraints, and other database constraints to maintain data accuracy and integrity.

This project demonstrates real-world business operations by using SQL joins to retrieve meaningful information, such as customer order history, product sales, and payment details.

The project also uses custom functions, triggers, and stored procedures to automate tasks and handle different business requirements.

The main purpose of this project is to demonstrate important DBMS concepts, including constraints, relationships, joins, functions, triggers, and stored procedures, while developing a practical E-commerce Management System.
